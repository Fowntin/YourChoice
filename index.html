    Frequently Asked Questions

    Fast answers regarding orders, delivery, payments & policies

    How quickly will my order be delivered?

    Orders within Greater Accra and Tema are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Shipments to regional capitals across Ghana (Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale, Sunyani, etc.) arrive within 2 to 4 business days with trackable parcel dispatch.

    What payment methods are supported?

    We accept all major Ghana Mobile Money networks (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), Visa & Mastercard debit/credit cards, and Cash or MoMo on Delivery in verified service zones.

    How do I track my active order?

    Click on My Orders in the top header or mobile menu. When logged in, your active delivery status and receipt breakdown update in real time.

    What is your warranty and 7-day return guarantee?

    All brand-new electronics carry manufacturer warranties. In addition, YourChoice provides a 7-day hassle-free return or replacement window for any verified defective merchandise.

    How is my private data and payment info secured?

    We utilize bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store payment card PINs or security codes on our servers, and customer contact information is never sold to third parties.

    Can I pick up my package in person?

    Yes. Walk-in pickup is available at our East Legon Hub in Accra from Monday to Saturday between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM GMT.
