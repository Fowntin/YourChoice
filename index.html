Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will my order be delivered?

Deliveries within Accra and Tema are fulfilled within 24 to 48 hours. Orders to regional capitals across Ghana take between 2 to 4 business days with trackable dispatch.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), Visa & Mastercard card payments, and Cash/MoMo on Delivery in verified service zones.

How can I track my active orders?

Click on My Orders in the top navigation or mobile drawer. When logged into your account or with your order reference ID, you can view real-time fulfillment status.

What is your warranty and return policy?

All brand-new electronics come with standard manufacturer warranty. We offer a 7-day hassle-free return or replacement window for any verified defective merchandise.
